There is a version of this trip that lives only in the lab envelope, and a
version that lives in memory. They rarely agree, and that disagreement is the
reason I still shoot film.

## What I carried

One rangefinder, two lenses, more rolls than I needed and exactly the right
amount of patience. Weight is a moral question on a long climb. Every gram you
carry up the ridge is a gram you chose over something else.

## What survived

Most of it did not. That is the point. The frames that came back are the ones
worth keeping, and the rest taught me where I was standing when I got it wrong.
